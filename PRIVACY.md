# Privacy Policy: ThirdEyeFriends Publisher

ThirdEyeFriends Publisher is a private tool used only by the ThirdEyeFriends team to upload our own videos
(the animated series *The Mushroom Guy*) to our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

- **Who uses it:** only the account owner. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in.
- **What it accesses:** only what's needed to post to our own accounts: basic account info (name/ID) and permission to upload videos.
- **What it stores:** sign-in tokens, kept on our own computer to post on our behalf. No data about other users is collected, stored, sold or shared.
- **Removing access:** access can be revoked at any time in each platform's app settings.

Contact: via the @third.eye.friends accounts.

Last updated: 12 November 2026.
